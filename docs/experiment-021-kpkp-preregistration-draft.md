# Draft for review — Experiment 021 KPKP exact branch-law discovery

## Status: not frozen and not authorized to run

This document is a preregistration **draft**. It is not a frozen protocol,
benchmark, configuration, or authorization to download/probe new tablebase
outcomes. No KPKP tablebase outcome has been opened for this draft.

Before any exact outcome is probed, implementation must reproduce an
outcome-free census, canonicalization audit, partition counts, sample digests,
required tablebase-closure manifest, file byte counts, and SHA-256 identities.
Those values and the final reviewed protocol must then be committed together.

Experiments 001–020, including Experiment 020's failed temporal-union gate and
its stop decision, remain unchanged. No ordinary-position model training or
additional Stockfish budget tier is permitted.

## Scientific question

Can a compact, generic function of an exact position/move transition identify
a decision-sufficient subset of branches when branch importance is
mathematically known?

The target remains a branch function

```text
B(P, m) -> importance score or retention decision
```

and, eventually, a computation-allocation function

```text
D(P, m) -> additional computation assigned to the branch
```

Experiment 021 tests one-ply exact branch prediction only. Recursive branch
selection remains prohibited until an unchanged one-ply candidate passes
separate exact selection, transfer, and confirmation experiments.

## Primary domain

Use **KPKP**: one white king and pawn versus one black king and pawn. It is the
strongest first four-piece laboratory because it contains opposing pawn races,
king opposition, zugzwang, captures, en passant, promotion threats, and quiet
tempo moves while remaining exactly solved by Syzygy.

Experiment 021 must not merely repeat Experiment 015's three-piece WDL curve.
KPKP is intended to expose states in which only one move wins, draws, or avoids
loss and states in which several outcome-equivalent moves differ in exact
zeroing progress.

The proposed first transfer domain is **KPPvK** because it preserves the
king/pawn primitive vocabulary while changing the strategic structure from an
opposed race to same-side pawn coordination. KRvKP remains the later
cross-piece transfer candidate. Neither transfer outcome is opened in
Experiment 021.

## Exact state definition

Enumerate fixed-material standard-chess states satisfying all of the following:

- exactly one white king, one white pawn, one black king, and one black pawn;
- both sides to move;
- pawns only on ranks two through seven;
- no castling rights;
- halfmove clock fixed at zero and fullmove number normalized;
- all boards pass `python-chess` validity checks; and
- legal en-passant rights are included as distinct rule states only when a
  legal immediately preceding double-pawn move can produce them.

Retain legal terminal states in the outcome-free census and exclude them from
branch prediction. Record no-en-passant and en-passant states as separate
outcome-free strata.

### Valid symmetries

Canonicalize the complete rule state under the group generated by:

1. identity;
2. file reflection;
3. 180-degree rotation plus color swap; and
4. file reflection composed with 180-degree rotation plus color swap.

The transform must include side to move and en-passant square. Pawn direction
must be preserved after color normalization. Canonicalization tests must prove
that every transformed position has the same oriented legal-move structure.
No rank reflection without color swap is valid.

The canonical key includes piece squares, side to move, and en-passant state.
The two pawns are color-distinct. A canonical symmetry orbit belongs wholly to
one data partition.

## Outcome-free census and partitions

Before tablebase probing:

1. enumerate the complete legal KPKP census;
2. canonicalize every state and audit orbit closure;
3. assign canonical classes by seeded SHA-256 to 60% development, 20%
   selection, and 20% confirmation;
4. freeze raw, canonical, terminal, en-passant, and partition counts;
5. freeze SHA-256 digests for every partition and deterministic sample; and
6. verify zero canonical-key overlap among partitions.

Proposed deterministic samples are:

- 100,000 nonterminal development states;
- 25,000 nonterminal selection states; and
- 25,000 nonterminal confirmation states.

Sampling is by SHA-256 rank within partition, with side-to-move and en-passant
coverage fixed using outcome-free strata. Primary population metrics use
inverse sampling weights if structural strata are sampled disproportionately.
Challenge-stratum metrics are reported separately.

Experiment 021 may probe and train on the development sample only. Selection
and confirmation FENs, exact labels, branch records, and aggregates must be
stored behind separate commands and paths that the Experiment 021 command
cannot open. Their identities may be audited outcome-free; their tablebase
outcomes remain sealed.

## Exact tablebase truth and provenance

Use local Syzygy WDL50 and DTZ50 through `python-chess`. Before probing, freeze
the complete transitive tablebase closure required by KPKP successors,
including captures, underpromotions, and promotions into queen, rook, bishop,
or knight domains. Every file must have a frozen URL, byte count, SHA-256, and
manifest identity.

Fail closed for a missing file, checksum difference, illegal state, incomplete
successor, recurrence mismatch, or unexpected probe. Tablebase values may
appear only in labels and evaluation metrics; they are forbidden from model
inputs, feature selection, branch ordering, and uncertainty estimation.

## Exact branch labels

For every nonterminal development state, probe the root and every legal
successor. Orient every move value to the root mover.

Define the five-valued WDL-optimal set

```text
O_WDL(P) = {m : WDL(P, m) = max_j WDL(P, j)}
```

and the DTZ-optimal subset

```text
O_DTZ(P) = argmin_{m in O_WDL(P)} DTZ(P, m)
```

using the frozen Syzygy one-ply recurrence and deterministic lexical ties.

Store at least:

- root WDL50 and DTZ50;
- successor and mover-oriented WDL50/DTZ50 for every legal move;
- membership in `O_WDL` and `O_DTZ`;
- exact WDL and DTZ rank/tie group;
- count of WDL-optimal and DTZ-optimal moves;
- whether the move is the unique WDL-optimal branch;
- whether the move is the unique DTZ-optimal branch;
- whether a non-losing state has exactly one move preserving its WDL category
  (`unique_saving`);
- WDL degradation if omitted;
- move and transition primitives; and
- tablebase manifest and label-definition identities.

### Necessary versus redundant branches

A branch is **individually WDL-necessary** exactly when it is the sole member
of `O_WDL`. When `|O_WDL| > 1`, the optimal branches are redundant for one-ply
WDL safety: a retained set needs at least one member, not all members.

Report unique necessary branches separately from redundant WDL-optimal and
redundant DTZ-optimal branches. Complete optimal-set recall is diagnostic only
and is never substituted for decision sufficiency.

## Primitive representation

Candidate inputs may describe `P -> P_m` using only generic, locally computed
information:

- raw piece/square occupancy before and after;
- changed squares, moving piece, source, destination, capture, and promotion;
- side to move and en-passant availability;
- attack and defense maps before and after;
- legal-move counts before and after;
- king and pawn distance relationships;
- local geometry, rays, blockers, adjacency, and promotion distance; and
- differences of these quantities across the transition.

Human-named concepts such as opposition, zugzwang, breakthrough, key square,
outside passer, or corresponding squares are forbidden as learner inputs in
Experiment 021. They may be considered only as post-result interpretations.

Tablebase WDL, DTZ, ranks, optimal-set size, or any transform of an exact label
is forbidden as input. A candidate's uncertainty may use only its own score
margin, sparse-model resampling on development folds, or disagreement among
frozen compact candidates.

## Baselines and compact candidates

Every method produces either a complete deterministic move ordering or a
deterministic adaptive retained set. Compare at equal branch budgets of top 1,
2, 3, 4, 5, 8, and all legal moves:

1. deterministic random ordering with frozen repeats;
2. forcing-category priority then lexical fill;
3. checks, all captures, promotions, and evasions then lexical fill;
4. Locked-3 successor ranking;
5. primitive sparse logistic/ranking model, capped at 64 nonzero coefficients;
6. primitive decision tree, capped at depth five and 31 internal nodes;
7. primitive rule list or symbolic expression, capped at 16 clauses/nodes;
8. compact-model uncertainty set using only frozen score margins or
   development-fold disagreement; and
9. oracle WDL then DTZ as a nondeployable upper reference.

A diagnostic nonlinear model of at most 2,048 scalar parameters may estimate
whether the primitive representation contains learnable signal, but it cannot
advance as the discovered law in Experiment 021.

Training and hyperparameter choice use nested folds entirely inside the
development partition, grouped by canonical symmetry class. All candidate
families, feature computation, caps, tie rules, folds, and the final nomination
rule must be committed before any KPKP label is probed.

## Primary measurements

For each method, budget, and prespecified stratum report:

- pooled and mean-context retained fraction;
- fraction of states retaining at least one member of `O_WDL`;
- fraction retaining at least one member of `O_DTZ`;
- unique-WDL-necessary branch recall;
- unique-DTZ-optimal branch recall;
- unique-saving branch recall;
- WDL degradation distribution and catastrophic WDL loss count;
- complete optimal-set recall as a diagnostic;
- mean legal and retained branches;
- model parameters, nonzero terms, serialized bytes, source size;
- inference operations, memory, and latency; and
- results separately for wins, cursed wins, draws, blessed losses, losses,
  side to move, en-passant states, unique-saving states, redundant-optimal
  states, captures, promotions, quiet moves, and prespecified primitive geometry
  strata defined without outcome-derived chess concepts.

The primary decision metric is

```text
ExactDecisionPreservation(S, P) = 1 if S(P) intersects O_WDL(P), else 0
```

not recall of every equivalent move.

Define development `rho_perfect` as the smallest observed retained fraction at
which every held-out development-fold state preserves exact five-valued WDL.
It is a development statistic, not a confirmation claim.

## Draft nomination gate

At most one compact candidate may be nominated for a separately frozen KPKP
selection experiment. On every held-out development fold it must:

- preserve exact five-valued WDL in 100% of states;
- recall 100% of unique-WDL-necessary and unique-saving branches;
- produce zero catastrophic WDL degradation in every prespecified stratum;
- achieve at least 95% DTZ-optimal retention;
- reduce `rho_perfect` by at least 25% relative to the best deployable baseline;
- use pooled retained fraction at most 25% and mean-context fraction at most
  30%;
- fit one of the advancing description caps above; and
- lie on the observed description-size/quality Pareto frontier.

If no candidate passes, record the negative result and do not inspect selection
or confirmation outcomes. Do not relax exact WDL safety after seeing results.

If one candidate passes, select by lower `rho_perfect`, then higher
DTZ-optimal retention, then fewer unique-saving errors, then smaller serialized
description, then lower inference operations, with lexical model-family ties.
Freeze that exact candidate before any selection probe.

## Later evidence sequence — not part of Experiment 021

1. **KPKP selection:** evaluate the frozen candidate once on the untouched
   25,000-state selection sample under the same exact safety gate.
2. **Four-piece transfer:** without refitting, test the unchanged primitive law
   first on KPPvK development/selection, then consider KRvKP as a cross-piece
   transfer.
3. **Exact confirmation:** freeze all choices and evaluate once on untouched
   KPKP and transfer-domain confirmation samples.
4. **Recursive test:** only after one-ply exact confirmation passes, apply the
   unchanged selector recursively and measure exact WDL preservation against
   total retained tree states.

A compact rule that passes KPKP, transfers unchanged, and survives exact
confirmation would be the project's first candidate formula-discovery result.
It is not called a breakthrough until that sequence is complete.

## Failure interpretation

Failure to compress KPKP under exact WDL safety is scientifically meaningful.
It would show that the apparent ordinary-position compression does not easily
generalize even to a four-piece solved domain. It would not justify returning
to larger Stockfish budgets, adding handcrafted KPKP concepts, opening sealed
partitions, or recursively applying a failed selector.

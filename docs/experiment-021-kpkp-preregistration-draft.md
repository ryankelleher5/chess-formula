## Scientific question

Can a compact, generic function of an exact position/move transition identify
a decision-sufficient subset of branches when branch importance is
mathematically known?

The target remains a branch function

```text
B(P, m) -> importance score or retention decision
```

and, eventually, a computation-allocation function

```text
D(P, m) -> additional computation assigned to the branch
```

Experiment 021 tests one-ply exact branch prediction only. Recursive branch
selection remains prohibited until an unchanged one-ply candidate passes
separate exact selection, transfer, and confirmation experiments.

## Primary domain

Use **KPKP**: one white king and pawn versus one black king and pawn. It is the
strongest first four-piece laboratory because it contains opposing pawn races,
king opposition, zugzwang, captures, en passant, promotion threats, and quiet
tempo moves while remaining exactly solved by Syzygy.

Experiment 021 must not merely repeat Experiment 015's three-piece WDL curve.
KPKP is intended to expose states in which only one move wins, draws, or avoids
loss and states in which several outcome-equivalent moves differ in exact
zeroing progress.

The proposed first transfer domain is **KPPvK** because it preserves the
king/pawn primitive vocabulary while changing the strategic structure from an
opposed race to same-side pawn coordination. KRvKP remains the later
cross-piece transfer candidate. Neither transfer outcome is opened in
Experiment 021.

## Exact state definition

Enumerate fixed-material standard-chess states satisfying all of the following:

- exactly one white king, one white pawn, one black king, and one black pawn;
- both sides to move;
- pawns only on ranks two through seven;
- no castling rights;
- halfmove clock fixed at zero and fullmove number normalized;
- all boards pass `python-chess` validity checks; and
- raw history enumeration records an en-passant marker when a legal immediately
  preceding double-pawn move can produce it; and
- decision-state identity retains that marker only when
  `board.has_legal_en_passant()` is true. A reachable marker that enables no
  legal capture is normalized to `-` before canonicalization, partitioning,
  folds, sampling, features, and transition isolation.

Retain legal terminal states in the outcome-free census and exclude them from
branch prediction. Record no-en-passant and **legally actionable** en-passant
states as separate outcome-free strata. Raw history twins may be counted for
audit purposes but never become two learning observations.

### Valid symmetries

Canonicalize the complete rule state under the group generated by:

1. identity;
2. file reflection;
3. 180-degree rotation plus color swap; and
4. file reflection composed with 180-degree rotation plus color swap.

The transform must include side to move and the normalized legal en-passant square. Pawn direction
must be preserved after color normalization. Canonicalization tests must prove
that every transformed position has the same oriented legal-move structure.
No rank reflection without color swap is valid.

The canonical key includes piece squares, side to move, and actionable en-passant state.
The two pawns are color-distinct. A canonical symmetry orbit belongs wholly to
one data partition.

## Exact branch labels

For every nonterminal development state, probe the root and every legal
successor. Orient every move value to the root mover.

Define the five-valued WDL-optimal set

```text
O_WDL(P) = {m : WDL(P, m) = max_j WDL(P, j)}
```

and the DTZ-optimal subset only after constructing mover-oriented move DTZ
through Experiment 015's validated recurrence:

```text
move_wdl = -successor_wdl
move_dtz = {2:1, 1:101, 0:0, -1:-101, -2:-1}[move_wdl]
           when the move is zeroing or terminal
move_dtz = -successor_dtz + 1 when move_wdl > 0 and nonzeroing
move_dtz = -successor_dtz - 1 when move_wdl < 0 and nonzeroing
move_dtz = 0                  when move_wdl = 0
O_DTZ(P) = argmin_{m in O_WDL(P)} move_dtz(P, m)
```

The WDL filter occurs before the DTZ comparison. A raw signed DTZ argmin across
different WDL categories is forbidden. Lexical order breaks only exact WDL and
derived-move-DTZ ties.

Store at least:

- root WDL50 and DTZ50;
- successor and mover-oriented WDL50/DTZ50 for every legal move;
- membership in `O_WDL` and `O_DTZ`;
- exact WDL and DTZ rank/tie group;
- count of WDL-optimal and DTZ-optimal moves;
- whether the move is the unique WDL-optimal branch;
- whether the move is the unique DTZ-optimal branch;
- whether a non-losing state has exactly one move preserving its WDL category
  (`unique_saving`);
- WDL degradation if omitted;
- move and transition primitives; and
- tablebase manifest and label-definition identities.

### Necessary versus redundant branches

A branch is **individually WDL-necessary** exactly when it is the sole member
of `O_WDL`. When `|O_WDL| > 1`, the optimal branches are redundant for one-ply
WDL safety: a retained set needs at least one member, not all members.

Report unique necessary branches separately from redundant WDL-optimal and
redundant DTZ-optimal branches. Complete optimal-set recall is diagnostic only
and is never substituted for decision sufficiency.

## Frozen transferable primitive representation

Every transition is first canonicalized jointly with its move under the four
valid transforms. The implemented vector is fixed-length and color-relative,
so it accepts zero, one, or multiple pieces of every type per color. It never
assumes exactly one pawn per side.

The complete proposed vocabulary is:

- 12 binary occupancy planes before and 12 after: self/opponent × pawn,
  knight, bishop, rook, queen, king × 64 squares;
- four binary union-attack planes: self/opponent before and after × 64 squares;
- one-hot moving piece over six types;
- one-hot captured piece over none plus six types, with en-passant encoded as a
  captured pawn;
- one-hot promotion over none, knight, bishop, rook, and queen;
- source and destination file/rank divided by seven;
- capture, gives-check, zeroing, and legally actionable en-passant flags;
- legal-move counts before and after divided by 218;
- self and opponent pawn counts divided by eight; and
- minimum self/opponent pawn promotion distance divided by six, using one when
  that side has no pawn.

No other input is permitted. Binary features are unchanged. Continuous
features use exactly the normalizations above; sparse/tree/symbolic candidates
receive no outcome-dependent standardization. Before labels are opened, tests
must prove that every valid transformed `(P,m)` produces an identical feature
vector and that transformed move scores/orderings correspond exactly. The
implemented regression suite covers legal EP captures, irrelevant EP twins,
promotions, ordinary captures, Black-to-move positions, and KPPvK transforms
with two same-colored pawns.

Human-named concepts such as opposition, zugzwang, breakthrough, key square,
outside passer, or corresponding squares are forbidden as learner inputs in
Experiment 021. They may be considered only as post-result interpretations.

Tablebase WDL, DTZ, ranks, optimal-set size, or any transform of an exact label
is forbidden as input. A candidate's uncertainty may use only its own score
margin or disagreement among the exact inner/outer models permitted for the
current cross-fit or deployment stage.

## Baselines and compact candidates

Every method produces either a complete deterministic move ordering or a
deterministic adaptive retained set. Compare at equal branch budgets of top 1,
2, 3, 4, 5, 8, and all legal moves:

1. deterministic random ordering with repeats `0..19`; each move is ordered by
   ascending SHA-256 hex of UTF-8
   `random-v1:{20260908}:{repeat}:{canonical_state_key}:{move_uci}`, then UCI;
2. forcing-category priority then lexical fill;
3. checks, all captures, promotions, and evasions then lexical fill;
4. Locked-3 successor ranking;
5. the frozen sparse logistic ranker below;
6. the frozen decision tree below;
7. the frozen symbolic score below;
8. compact-model uncertainty set using only frozen score margins or
   development-fold disagreement; and
9. oracle WDL then DTZ as a nondeployable upper reference.

There is no diagnostic nonlinear model in Experiment 021. Adding one requires
a new outcome-free reviewed protocol rather than an exploratory side channel.

### Sparse logistic ranker

Fit binary `wdl_optimal` with position weight `1/legal_moves(P)` and a positive
class multiplier equal to `negative_weight/positive_weight` inside each
training fold. Use scikit-learn 1.9.0 `LogisticRegression`, SAGA,
`l1_ratio=1.0`, intercept enabled, tolerance `1e-6`, maximum 20,000 iterations,
seed `20260908`, and
`C in {2^-8, 2^-6, 2^-4, 2^-2, 1, 2^2, 2^4, 2^6, 2^8}`. Reject a fit that
does not converge or has more than 64 nonzero coefficients after rounding.
All scikit-learn fits run inside `threadpoolctl==3.6.0` with a one-thread limit.

### Decision tree

Use deterministic CART with Gini impurity, best splitter, the same sample/class
weights, seed `20260908`, `max_depth in {2,3,4,5}`, and
`min_samples_leaf in {32,128,512}`. Cap the chosen tree at 31 internal nodes.
No pruning or split threshold may be edited after results.

### Symbolic score

Enumerate exactly 4,096 expressions using `symbolic-v1`: expression index `i`
is decoded from `SHA256("symbolic:20260908:{i}")`. Terminals are feature indices
0–1,823. Permitted operators are binary add, subtract, multiply, minimum, and
maximum plus unary absolute value and negation. The four fixed decoded forms
have one to four AST nodes; constants, division, conditionals, transcendental
functions, labels, and manual expressions are forbidden.

Screen on the first 4,096 outer-training positions ordered by
`SHA256("symbolic-screen:20260908:" + canonical_key)`. Rank by fewer states
whose maximum-score move set omits `O_WDL`, lower mean rank of the first
`O_WDL` move, smaller canonical serialization, lexical serialization, then
expression index. Re-evaluate the best 16 on the complete outer-training data
with the same order. This algorithm and its canonical AST serializer are
implemented before labels are available.

### Nested folds, serialization, and uncertainty set

Use five outer and four inner development folds. One-ply-connected roots are
unioned before assignment, so no transition component crosses a fold. For each
held-out outer fold `k`, generate a separate four-fold inner assignment over
only the other four outer folds. All five inner maps, counts, and digests are
frozen in the review config.

Evaluation is strictly cross-fitted. For outer fold `k`:

1. each inner validation fold is scored only by a model trained on the other
   inner folds inside the outer-training portion;
2. choose family hyperparameters and `delta` from those inner cross-fitted
   predictions by zero WDL errors, lower pooled retention, higher
   population-weighted DTZ retention, smaller complete ensemble description,
   then lexical identifier;
3. if no setting has zero inner error, mark that family
   `failed_no_zero_error_setting`; retain the setting with fewest errors, lower
   pooled retention, then lower `delta` for fixed diagnostics only;
4. fit one outer-training-full model and four inner models, each trained wholly
   inside outer training; and
5. score outer fold `k` using the full model's margin union the top-move votes
   of those four inner models. Every contributing fit excludes outer fold `k`.

Only after all five outer evaluations are pooled may one candidate family and
setting be nominated. The later sealed-selection artifact is then fitted as a
six-model ensemble: one full-development model supplies margins and five
outer-fold models supply votes. No full-development score or vote may appear
in development evaluation. Deployment hyperparameters are the modal setting
selected by the five outer-inner procedures, with canonical lexical JSON ties;
deployment `delta` is the maximum of the five outer-selected deltas. This rule
is fixed even if a different aggregation would look better post hoc.

Rank moves by score descending and UCI lexical ties. Store coefficients and
thresholds rounded to six decimal places in separator-free, sorted-key UTF-8
JSON. Re-load that rounded artifact and recompute every reported prediction;
in-memory pre-rounding predictions are forbidden. Serialized size includes the
shared feature-schema identifier/count/normalization block plus every model's
coefficients, intercept, tree nodes or AST, thresholds, and hyperparameters.
The feature-name generator is counted in separately reported source bytes.
Operation counts include every feature read, comparison, addition,
multiplication, min/max, absolute value, logistic exponential/division, vote,
margin, and retained-set union comparison.

Description size, parameters, operations, memory, and latency cover the entire
five-model outer-evaluation ensemble or six-model deployed ensemble as
applicable. They may never be reported per fit. Deployment latency is measured
end to end across all six fits and the union operation for 1,000 repetitions
per reported position with no discarded warmup; report mean and median
nanoseconds plus the captured hardware/software environment.

For logistic regression and trees, the normalized score is the serialized
artifact's predicted probability. For symbolic scores, use within-position
min-max normalization; when all legal moves tie, every normalized score is
one. The only adaptive deployed uncertainty set retains the full-development
model's top move plus: (a) every move ranked top by at least two of the five
outer-fold models, and (b) every move whose full-model probability/normalized
score is within `delta` of the state best for
`delta in {0,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.10,0.20}`. Choose `delta` by the same inner-fold
rule. No variance statistic, extra ensemble, or post-result threshold is
allowed.

## Primary measurements

For each method, budget, and prespecified stratum report:

- pooled and mean-context retained fraction;
- fraction of states retaining at least one member of `O_WDL`;
- fraction retaining at least one member of `O_DTZ`;
- unique-WDL-necessary branch recall;
- unique-DTZ-optimal branch recall;
- unique-saving branch recall;
- WDL degradation distribution and catastrophic WDL loss count;
- complete optimal-set recall as a diagnostic;
- mean legal and retained branches;
- model parameters, nonzero terms, serialized bytes, source size;
- inference operations, memory, and latency; and
- results separately for wins, cursed wins, draws, blessed losses, losses,
  side to move, en-passant states, unique-saving states, redundant-optimal
  states, captures, promotions, and quiet moves; and
- outcome-free primitive strata fixed as legal-move count `1–4/5–8/9+`, king
  Chebyshev distance `2/3–4/5+`, pawn-file distance `0/1/2–3/4+`, minimum pawn
  promotion distance `1/2/3–4/5–6`, legal-EP `false/true`, and move kind
  `quiet/capture/promotion/check/legal-EP`. Overlapping move-kind flags are
  each reported; none is made mutually exclusive after outcomes.

The primary decision metric is

```text
ExactDecisionPreservation(S, P) = 1 if S(P) intersects O_WDL(P), else 0
```

not recall of every equivalent move.

For a complete ordering, define development `rho_perfect` by taking, in every
outer-held-out state, the minimal prefix ending at its first `O_WDL` member and
then computing the development-population-weighted pooled fraction
`sum_i w_i prefix_i / sum_i w_i legal_i`. For an adaptive set, use its actual
retained counts and require zero WDL errors. Also report weighted mean-context
and unweighted sample fractions, but neither replaces this primary estimand.
It is a cross-fitted development statistic, not a confirmation claim.

DTZ retention is the development-population-weighted mean of the per-state
indicator `S(P) intersects O_DTZ(P)`; its denominator is total development
population weight, never moves or only-WDL-success states. Equal-budget DTZ
comparison gives each baseline the candidate's exact retained count in each
position. The best deployable baseline is chosen only among forcing,
all-captures, and Locked-3 by lower `rho_perfect`, higher weighted DTZ
retention, smaller serialized bytes, fewer operations, then lexical ID. Random
and oracle references cannot set the advancement baseline.

## Draft nomination gate

At most one compact candidate may be nominated for a separately frozen KPKP
selection experiment. On every held-out development fold it must:

- preserve exact five-valued WDL in 100% of states;
- recall 100% of unique-WDL-necessary and unique-saving branches;
- produce zero catastrophic WDL degradation in every prespecified stratum;
- match or exceed the best deployable baseline's DTZ-optimal retention under
  the candidate's exact per-position retained counts;
- reduce `rho_perfect` by at least 25% relative to the best deployable baseline;
- use pooled retained fraction at most 25% and mean-context fraction at most
  30%;
- fit one of the advancing description caps above; and
- lie on the observed description-size/quality Pareto frontier.

If no candidate passes, record the negative result and do not inspect selection
or confirmation outcomes. Do not relax exact WDL safety after seeing results.

If one candidate passes, select by lower `rho_perfect`, then higher
DTZ-optimal retention, then fewer unique-saving errors, then smaller serialized
description, then lower inference operations, with lexical model-family ties.
Freeze that exact candidate before any selection probe.

A passing candidate with at least 95% DTZ-optimal retention additionally earns
the descriptive label **distance-aware candidate**. Falling below 95% cannot
veto an otherwise perfect-WDL candidate when it still matches or beats the
best deployable DTZ baseline.

## Later evidence sequence — not part of Experiment 021

1. **KPKP selection:** evaluate the frozen candidate once on the untouched
   25,000-state selection sample under the same exact safety gate.
2. **Four-piece transfer:** without refitting, test the unchanged primitive law
   first on KPPvK development/selection, then consider KRvKP as a cross-piece
   transfer.
3. **Exact confirmation:** freeze all choices and evaluate once on untouched
   KPKP and transfer-domain confirmation samples.
4. **Recursive test:** only after one-ply exact confirmation passes, apply the
   unchanged selector recursively and measure exact WDL preservation against
   total retained tree states.

A compact rule that passes KPKP, transfers unchanged, and survives exact
confirmation would be the project's first candidate formula-discovery result.
It is not called a breakthrough until that sequence is complete.

## Failure interpretation

Failure to compress KPKP under exact WDL safety is scientifically meaningful.
It would show that the apparent ordinary-position compression does not easily
generalize even to a four-piece solved domain. It would not justify returning
to larger Stockfish budgets, adding handcrafted KPKP concepts, opening sealed
partitions, or recursively applying a failed selector.
